# 1. Minimal Style

[![Click to view example](https://www.ogis.org/examples/outdoor/style-only/screenshot.png)](https://www.ogis.org/examples/outdoor/style-only/#12.95/45.81932/11.74289)

[View Example](https://www.ogis.org/examples/outdoor/style-only/#12.95/45.81932/11.74289)

Intended as a demonstration of a minimal "activity" (cycling) starting point and by no means exhaustive.

Looking for low hanging fruit that gets the most out of data already contained in the tiles. Plus DEM data.

- Uses Liberty as a base (upstream actively maintained).
- Adds hillshading/exaggeration (raster DEM from Mapterhorn).
- Increases visibility of all **paths** (and path labels) at all zooms (was white, now red dotted).
- Adds a _minimal_ cycling example:
  - MTB difficulty (blue/red/black) [mtb:scale](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:mtb:scale) [taken from this comment](https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap/issues/31#issuecomment-4649028862)
  - Increase visibility of bicycle access tags (purple).

This helped give me a better understanding of the activity based data available. For cycling I noticed that different rating systems exist like [mtb:scale:imba](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Key:mtb:scale:imba) and are not supported. I can only imagine the number of combinations of tags used by different activities!

# 3. Waymarked Trails

[![Click to view example](https://www.ogis.org/examples/outdoor/style-contours-waymarked/screenshot.png)](https://www.ogis.org/examples/outdoor/style-contours-waymarked/#12.95/45.81932/11.74289)

[View Example](https://www.ogis.org/examples/outdoor/style-contours-waymarked/#12.95/45.81932/11.74289)

To better understand what goes into route rendering, I looked to [Waymarked Trails: Hiking](https://hiking.waymarkedtrails.org/#help-about). Lots of useful links and information there.

Their "waymarked" routes are available as PNGs on a transparent background. Adding this as a raster source adds route information unavailable to us through the OFM tiles.

In this example, the required relation-level tags (e.g. [ref=190, network=lwn](https://www.openstreetmap.org/relation/6457315)) are not currently available to display the numbered/red waymarked trails. Instead, they are displayed the same as any other path (red dotted).

Taking this further, I decided to all ALL activity layers available through waymarkedtrails ('hiking', 'cycling', 'mtb', 'skating', 'riding', 'slopes')... because why not?!
